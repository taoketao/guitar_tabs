                  She Talks To Angels - Black Crowes
----------------------------------------------------------------------
 
Transcribed by Derreck
 
 
This is an awesome song on the acoustic, sounds good alone and even better with
two guitars. You'll have to listen to the song for the rhythm (obviously). If you're a
beginner, e-mail me and I'll send you a recording of how I do the intro and chorus
to get you rolling.
 
This song is in Open E tuning. Tune your 5th string (2nd from top) up a whole step
from A to B, 4th string up a whole step from D to E, and your 3rd string a half
step from G to G#.
 
      h  - hammer on
      p  - pull off
      b  - bend string
      r  - release bend
      s  - slide
      v  - downward strum
      ^  - upward strum
      NH - Natural Harmonics
